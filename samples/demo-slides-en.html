Gradient Descent & Learning Rate

Week 4

1

Gradient Descent

  • Update weights in the direction that lowers the loss function
  • Learning rate: step size of each update
  • Too large → overshoots the minimum, loss may diverge
  • Too small → slow training, may not converge within the epochs
2

Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD)

  • Compute the gradient on a mini-batch instead of the full dataset
  • Faster updates per step
  • Gradient noise can help escape shallow local minima
3

Learning Rate Scheduling

  • Step decay: halve the learning rate every few epochs
  • Cosine annealing: decrease along a cosine curve
  • Warmup: start small and increase the learning rate during the first 5 epochs
